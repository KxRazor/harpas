# Code of conduct

Harpas is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. This code of conduct
applies to every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other place where
someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a language that is not their own.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Disagree with arguments, and accept that a decision may not go your way.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong and, when you can, how to improve it.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding that someone meant harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address, email or real name, without their consent.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, and repeatedly pushing a topic after maintainers asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide how to respond, in proportion to what happened: a private or public warning, hiding or
editing content, locking a conversation, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who do
not follow this code may be removed from their role.
