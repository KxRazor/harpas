- Block drops and pickup: blocks broken in Survival, and blocks that break with them, drop their loot as vanilla's
  block loot tables give it, drawing from the world's random sequences, and players pick up the items they walk
  over into their inventory. Enchantments are read as none and the data loot functions copy into items is not
  kept yet, so a shulker box drops without its contents (`harpas-engine`).
