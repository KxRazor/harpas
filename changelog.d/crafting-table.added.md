- Crafting tables: players open their 3x3 menu and craft the vanilla data pack's shaped and shapeless recipes,
  with inventory clicks, shift-crafting and bucket remainders. Closing the menu, moving away or losing the
  table returns ingredients to the inventory and drops what does not fit; disconnecting drops the grid and
  cursor. Menu slots, recipes and shift-clicks are compared with vanilla (`harpas-engine`, `harpas-server`).
