- Placing blocks: players in Creative fill their inventory and hotbar, and place blocks as vanilla's block items
  place them, with reach and room checks, neighbor, shape and light updates and acknowledgements. Blocks without
  properties, pillars, slabs and double slabs, stairs, blocks that face the player, and fences, walls, panes and
  bars are placed so far; in Survival a placed block takes one from the stack. Item stacks are sent and received
  with their data components kept as bytes, and the item registry of Java 26.1 is in `harpas-core`
  (`harpas-engine`, `harpas-protocol-java`, `harpas-core`).
