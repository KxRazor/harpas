- Inventory and crafting: players in Survival move items in their inventory with every click vanilla has (picking
  up, splitting, shift-clicking, number keys, dragging, gathering, throwing), wear armor in its slots, and craft
  in the inventory's 2x2 grid with the shaped and shapeless recipes of the vanilla data pack. The client's
  predictions are checked and only what it got wrong is sent again (`harpas-engine`, `harpas-protocol-java`).
