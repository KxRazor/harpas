- Dropped items: players drop items with the drop key and out of the Creative inventory, and the items fall,
  slide, float, burn in lava, merge with the stacks around them and despawn after five minutes as vanilla's item
  entities do, seen by the players within 96 blocks. Entities live in the storage the engine's design describes,
  and Set Entity Metadata is written by hand (`harpas-engine`, `harpas-protocol-java`).
